Features

Built around your documents

A flexible set of tools for working with PDFs on iPad, from marking up a page to reorganizing an entire document.

✍️

Apple Pencil notes

Write, mark up, and highlight directly over your PDF pages.

🗂️

Page organizer

Browse page previews, move or duplicate pages, and export selected pages.

🔍

Zoom and navigate

Explore documents using continuous scrolling, page navigation, and zoom controls.

🖼️

Images and annotations

Select, move, and resize compatible editable PDF annotations and image stamps.

📝

Text boxes

Add FreeText annotations and adjust their placement and size.

📤

PDF export

Save edited PDFs and choose whether to flatten annotations when exporting selected pages.
